About Us

Welcome to Saladific

At Saladific, we believe that healthy food should be delicious, convenient, and accessible to everyone. Founded by Sri Siddhivinayak Enterprise, our mission is to provide fresh, nutrient-packed meals that fuel your body and satisfy your taste buds.

Located in the heart of Satellite, Ahmedabad, we source the freshest local ingredients to create our salads, wraps, subs, and cold-pressed juices. Every item on our menu is carefully crafted to provide balanced nutrition without compromising on flavor.

Our commitment to quality and food safety is paramount. We maintain the highest standards in food preparation and hold a valid FSSAI license to ensure that every meal we serve is safe, hygienic, and of the best quality.
